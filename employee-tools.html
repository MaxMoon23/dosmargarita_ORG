🧭 Latitude / Longitude Finder

Look up the coordinates for a store so you can paste them into the Map pin block when adding it to the website. This runs entirely in your browser — nothing about this page touches Shopify or your login. It looks the address up using OpenStreetMap, a free public map database.

A full street address works best (e.g. "12391 Kingsride Ln, Houston, TX"). A store name + city usually works too (e.g. "Iris Gifts Decor, Houston TX"). A bare website link (like "irisgiftsanddecor.com") won't work — this tool can't visit outside websites to read their address for you, so grab the address text from the site's contact/footer section and paste that in instead.
